The Challenge

Great beans,
invisible brand.

Fernwood roasted some of the best single-origin coffee in the region — but online, none of that came through. Posts went up whenever someone had a spare minute, the voice drifted between playful and clinical, and the email list of 9,000 subscribers hadn't been mailed in four months.

They didn't need more tools. They needed a system: a consistent voice, a steady publishing cadence, and a way to turn each new roast into a week of content without hiring a full creative team.

Inconsistent voice No cadence Dormant email list Zero video
Before state — old feed / analytics screenshot · 4:5
03
The Approach

One engine,
four channels.

01

Locked the brand voice

Built a Client Brain — voice pillars, preferred words, and example posts — so every piece of copy sounds like Fernwood, not like a template.

02

Turned each roast into a content week

A single new bean now produces a launch post, three social clips, an email, and a story sequence — drafted, scored for the algorithm, and queued.

03

Revived the email list

Re-warmed 9,000 dormant subscribers with a story-led weekly digest tied to what's fresh on the roaster.

04

Added motion

Auto-generated short-form product videos from existing photography — no shoot required — feeding Reels and TikTok on a weekly beat.
