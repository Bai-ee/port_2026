# Blend decision — one account or several

Every bucket gets exactly one verdict. The **#1 goal** is the client's top-priority business goal; a bucket that feeds it is always **core**. Each kept bucket carries its goal's destination (link in the first self-reply).

| Verdict | Meaning | Brain result |
|---|---|---|
| **core** | Feeds the #1 goal (or a secondary goal whose buyers are the same audience) | Content Track + Track Target, largest share |
| **edge** | Different subject, but strengthens the positioning and is not hurt on this account | Content Track + Track Target, capped share (≤ 35% unless its own lift ≥ 1.0) |
| **own account** | Would grow better alone and/or pulls the main account toward the wrong audience | No track; recommendation in Strategy Document › Decisions / Open questions, with evidence |
| **park** | Too little content, or works against the #1 goal | No track; listed in Topics To Avoid if harmful, else Open questions |

## Signals (score each kept/rejected bucket on all six)

| # | Signal | Measured by | Pushes toward |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Goal fit** — can a follower won by this bucket reach a goal destination (hire, book, buy)? Name which goal. | judgment, stated in one sentence | yes → core/edge · no → own/park |
| 2 | **Audience overlap** with the #1-goal bucket | quote-target Jaccard + shared interactions (`bucket-analysis.md` §4) | high → edge · low → own account |
| 3 | **Own-account lift** | bucket avg likes / account avg | ≥ 0.8 → keep · < 0.5 with n ≥ 10 → own/park |
| 4 | **Positioning fit** — does it make the one-line positioning sharper or blurrier? | judgment against the positioning line | sharper → edge |
| 5 | **Volume** — can inventory supply its share for 30 days? | content inventory count vs posts/day × 30 | short → park or lower share |
| 6 | **Algorithm cost** — off-topic posts teach ranking and followers the wrong audience | share of posts outside the #1-goal community | > 40% non-goal → split candidate |

## Rules of thumb

- A bucket that is **the client's unique edge** (an archive, a scene, a body of work nobody else in the #1-goal community has) defaults to **edge**, not own account, as long as signals 2–4 are not clearly negative. Uniqueness is the positioning.
- **Own account** needs at least two negative signals among 1, 2, 3, plus enough volume (5) to run a second account. Otherwise it is edge-at-low-share or park.
- **A bucket feeding a secondary goal with a different buyer** (e.g. record buyers vs design clients) is the classic own-account candidate: it has its own conversion path, so splitting costs the main account nothing it needs. Still requires the evidence above.
- **Market talk / speculation / politics** on a work account is park unless it is the goal itself — measured as the worst lane wherever tested.
- Never more than 4 buckets; never more than 3 kept tracks on one account at Tier 1 (attention and volume split too thin).
- Shares: core ≥ 50% of authored posts; kept tracks sum to 100.
