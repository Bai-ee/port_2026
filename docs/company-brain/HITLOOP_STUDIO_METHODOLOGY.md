# HITLOOP Studio Methodology v1.0

## Populating a World-Class Client Brain

This methodology operates under `STUDIO_INTELLIGENCE_STANDARD.md`.

Locked rule: research is never runtime. Studio research produces evidence, evidence informs approved decisions, and approved decisions are published into `CLIENT_BRAIN.md`.

## Purpose

The purpose of HITLOOP Studio is not to generate deliverables.

Its purpose is to produce the highest quality Client Brain possible.

Everything downstream, including Creative Briefs, Marketing Insights, Email Digests, Social Strategy, Executive Briefs, Lead Generation, Post Me, and future automation, is only as good as the intelligence contained within the Client Brain.

The Client Brain is the product's approved runtime source of strategic truth.

Studio exists to populate it.

## Core Principle

We do not collect information.

We curate decision-grade intelligence.

Every piece of research must answer one question:

> Does this improve the quality of future decisions?

If the answer is no, it does not belong in the Client Brain.

If the information is useful but not yet approved, it belongs in the Studio Intelligence Workspace:

```text
CLIENT_NAME/
  CLIENT_BRAIN.md
  AUTHORITY_INTELLIGENCE.md
  MARKET_INTELLIGENCE.md
  DISCOVERY_INTELLIGENCE.md
  CONVERSATION_INTELLIGENCE.md
  CONTENT_LIBRARY.md
  RESEARCH/
```

`CLIENT_BRAIN.md` stores approved strategic conclusions. The other documents store evidence, working intelligence, and reusable knowledge.

## Studio Workflow

Every client follows the same high-level lifecycle.

```text
Discovery
  -> Research
  -> Validation
  -> Approved Decisions
  -> Client Brain
  -> Compiled Runtime
  -> HITLOOP Outputs
```

Our role ends once the Client Brain contains enough approved intelligence to consistently produce high-quality outputs.

## Intelligence Domains

Every Client Brain is populated through six intelligence domains.

Each domain should eventually reach a complete state.

- What do they do?
- What category should they own?
- What categories should they avoid?
- Who hires them?
- Why do clients choose them?
- What is their unique value?
- What problems do they solve?
- What transformation do they create?
- What industries do they serve?
- What language should always describe them?
- What language should never describe them?

## Research Standards

Every recommendation should be grounded in evidence.

Research should prioritize:

- Primary sources
- Official documentation
- Company websites
- Verified interviews
- Public case studies
- Demonstrated work
- Quantifiable outcomes

Avoid assumptions whenever possible.

Separate verified facts from informed recommendations.

## Decision Approval

Research does not automatically become Client Brain intelligence.

Every recommendation should pass through an approval step.

Questions:

- Is it true?
- Is it useful?
- Is it durable?
- Will it improve future outputs?
- Does it align with the client's desired positioning?

If approved, it becomes part of the Client Brain.

## Continuous Enrichment

The Client Brain is never considered finished.

Every project, campaign, conversation, case study, and client interaction is an opportunity to improve it.

Future work should focus on increasing the quality, not simply the quantity, of approved intelligence.

## Working Principle

Every Studio engagement should leave the client with a stronger Client Brain than when it began.

The quality of every HITLOOP output is a direct reflection of the quality of the Client Brain that powers it.

Our job is not to generate more content.

Our job is to create better understanding.
