# Content Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define what the client should say repeatedly and how it should sound.

## Questions To Answer

- What topics should the client own?
- What recurring series or formats fit the client?
- What voice rules are approved?
- What words, topics, or claims should be avoided?

## Recommended Sources

- Past posts
- Founder writing
- Brand guide
- Onboarding interview
- Competitor content
- High-performing card outputs promoted through feedback

## Evidence Requirements

Voice and content decisions should reflect approved examples, not generic tone adjectives alone.

## Decision Examples

- `content.pillars`: AI Integration Notes
- `recurringSeries`: Project Diaries
- `voice.toneSummary`: Short, direct, tactical
- `doNotSay`: revolutionary

## Common Mistakes

- Creating too many pillars.
- Defining voice as abstract adjectives without rules.
- Letting trend content override positioning.
