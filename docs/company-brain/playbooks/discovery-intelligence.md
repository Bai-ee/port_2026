# Discovery Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define where the client should be discovered and what the system should monitor.

## Questions To Answer

- What search terms should HITLOOP monitor?
- Which platforms matter most?
- Which communities, publications, podcasts, and events shape attention?
- Which directories, awards, hashtags, and watchlists should feed discovery?

## Recommended Sources

- Google and AI search
- LinkedIn/X search
- Reddit and community search
- Podcast directories
- Event/conference agendas
- Award/directories in the category
- Market Insights outputs

## Evidence Requirements

Each item should have a clear reason to affect search, watchlists, content angles, or lead-gen discovery. Return suggested decisions, not link dumps.

## Decision Examples

- `keywords`: Creative Systems Architecture
- `primaryPlatforms`: web, x, linkedin
- `communities`: Creative Technologists
- `publications`: Figma blog
- `watchLists`: KOL accounts to monitor

## Common Mistakes

- Duplicating raw research without choosing what matters.
- Adding platforms where the audience is not active.
- Treating one-off inspiration as a durable watchlist.
