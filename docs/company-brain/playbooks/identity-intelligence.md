# Identity Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define what the client is, who hires them, what they sell, and which category they should own or avoid.

## Questions To Answer

- What does the client actually do?
- Who is the primary buyer or audience?
- What should the market call this client?
- What labels create confusion or weak positioning?

## Recommended Sources

- Client website
- Onboarding interview
- LinkedIn/company profiles
- Sales materials
- Founder notes
- Existing offers and service pages

## Evidence Requirements

Use explicit client-approved language when available. If website language conflicts with founder/operator language, create a suggested decision and mark the conflict for approval.

## Decision Examples

- `categoryToOwn`: Creative Systems Architecture
- `categoryToAvoid`: Generic AI agency
- `hiredBy`: Startup founders
- `selling`: AI workflow design

## Common Mistakes

- Storing every raw description as equally true.
- Preserving vague category labels because they appeared on the website.
- Treating audience guesses as approved ICP.
