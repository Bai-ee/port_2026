# Opportunity Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define where commercial opportunity exists and which signals should shape outreach or strategy.

## Questions To Answer

- Which ICP segments are worth targeting?
- Which verticals or geographies matter?
- What qualifies or disqualifies a prospect?
- Which outreach angles are credible?

## Recommended Sources

- Founder/client interview
- Existing customer list
- CRM notes
- Lead Gen outputs
- Market Insights outputs
- Sales conversations
- Competitor customer pages

## Evidence Requirements

Opportunity decisions should be tied to the offer, authority, and audience. Do not promote a segment just because it is large.

## Decision Examples

- `icpSegments`: AI SaaS founders
- `verticals`: Developer tools
- `qualificationSignals`: adopting AI in creative operations
- `disqualifiers`: looking for a generic agency

## Common Mistakes

- Treating every possible buyer as ICP.
- Ignoring disqualifiers.
- Using outreach angles unsupported by authority proof.

## Fields Populated

- `decisions.intelligence.opportunity`
- `audience`
- `market`
- `offers`
- `leadGen` values in `decisionDrivers`

## Downstream Cards Affected

Lead Gen, Strategy Builder, Marketing Insights, Email Digest, sales copy.
