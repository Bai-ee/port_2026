# Market Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define the competitive and category landscape around the client.

## Questions To Answer

- Which competitors should be watched?
- Which adjacent competitors shape buyer expectations?
- Which category terms matter?
- Which thought leaders, tools, or startups influence the market?

## Recommended Sources

- Search results
- Competitor websites
- Social profiles
- Industry newsletters
- Market Insights outputs
- Analyst or industry reports

## Evidence Requirements

Competitors and category terms should be relevant to the client's actual offer and audience. Avoid broad lists that do not change downstream decisions.

## Decision Examples

- `directCompetitors`: Design engineering studios
- `adjacentCompetitors`: AI creative workflow consultants
- `thoughtLeaders`: KOL accounts to monitor
- `keywords`: Creative operations

## Common Mistakes

- Mixing aspirational peers with true competitors without labeling.
- Adding every interesting account as a KOL.
- Using category terms that buyers do not use.
