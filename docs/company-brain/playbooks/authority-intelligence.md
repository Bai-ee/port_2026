# Authority Intelligence Playbook

## Purpose

Define why the client has the right to say what they say.

## Questions To Answer

- What proof supports the client's positioning?
- What claims are allowed?
- What claims are prohibited or unsupported?
- What work history, projects, metrics, or outcomes create trust?

## Recommended Sources

- Case studies
- Portfolio
- Resume/career history
- Press and talks
- Testimonials
- Knowledge base uploads
- Founder interview

## Evidence Requirements

Claims need a source. Do not promote metrics, client names, or outcomes unless they are explicitly supported.

## Decision Examples

- `trustReasons`: 15+ years enterprise creative technology experience
- `allowedClaims`: Cross-platform brand and product systems experience
- `prohibitedClaims`: Guaranteed growth

## Common Mistakes

- Turning aspirations into claims.
- Using generic social proof language without evidence.
- Omitting prohibited claims.
