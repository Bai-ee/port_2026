So what volume actually buys

Eighteen posts are not eighteen shots at your feed. They are eighteen entries into different people’s sessions, each of whom sees one or two at full weight. Volume works by widening the number of independent auctions you enter — never by dominating any single feed.

What clustering actually looks like

1.96 vs 1.31Posts per occupied hour, his against yours. 73% of his posts land in an hour that already contains another of his posts, and his busiest hour held eleven. He is denser than you, not sparser.

So spacing is not the lever

Author Diversity decays same-author posts inside one ranked session — not inside a clock hour. Two posts sixty minutes apart mostly compete for different sessions anyway, and when they do share one, the second is discounted rather than discarded. It still reaches every viewer whose session missed the first.

What to do instead

Copy his shape, not a spacing rule: roughly two posts per active hour, and add hours as volume grows rather than deepening the ones you already use. At Tier 2 that is 8 posts across 4–5 hours. Volume is the lever; the hour count follows it.

Pruning — what each lever actually changes

The instinct is to clean the follow graph: cut the sports accounts, cut the politics, tighten the topic. Check the direction of the arrow first. Who you follow builds your consumer embedding — the feed you are served. Who follows and engages with you builds your producer embedding — the one that decides which strangers you are retrievable for. Unfollowing someone does not change how X classifies you as an author. The two towers are separate, and only one of them is your reach.

The one real link runs the other way. Your feed is your reaction pool — you quote and retweet what is in front of you, and a feed spanning five clusters produced 80 accounts amplified exactly once. The follow graph hurt you through what it fed you, never through what it labelled you. So the fix is not subtraction: build a private List of the design-engineering, web3-design and ai-tooling accounts you already amplify, open that list and nothing else when you sit down to post, and let the home feed stay personal. Keep the politics. Keep anyone you enjoy. Mute what you want out of working hours without spending a follow. Removing followers is the only lever here that touches distribution directly, it only moves one account at a time, and on an audience this inert it buys less than a single week of replying would.

The actual recommendation

Superseding the “never post twice in an hour” rule this page previously carried. Volume is the lever. Hour count is a consequence of volume, not a discipline to manage.

Do not ration posts per hour. He puts 73% of his posts into an hour that already contains one of his, and his busiest hour held eleven. Author Diversity discounts a second post inside one ranked session — it does not delete it, and it does nothing at all to the viewers whose session missed the first. Stacking costs you very little. Not posting costs you everything.

Mix the stack, do not repeat it. 80% of his multi-post hours contain more than one type. 63% of yours are retweets, and retweet → retweet is your single most common in-hour sequence — you are clustering the one format that cannot reach a stranger. If you stack, stack a quote-react next to a showcase, not two retweets.

Reply playbook

Read this before you start replying, because the obvious mental model is wrong. Replies from accounts a viewer does not follow are stripped by OONRetweetReplyFilter during pre-scoring — so your reply is not going to travel through the For You feed. It works through two other surfaces entirely: the conversation view under a post people are already reading, and the profile click that follows a reply worth reading. Optimise for those, not for reach.

Algorithm ledger

What is confirmed in X’s published ranking code, what is a community hypothesis carried in this repo’s profile, and what is not supported at all. Kept separate on purpose, so a guess never gets treated as a fact.

CONFIRMED
Author Diversity decay

“Each post after an author’s first is multiplied by a decaying factor, down to a floor.” Named in home-mixer/scorers/ranking_scorer.rs. This is why high volume works across sessions and fails within one.

CONFIRMED
OONRetweetReplyFilter

Removes reposts and replies from non-followed accounts during pre-scoring filtering. Retweets and replies cannot reach strangers through For You. Quote-tweets are not in this filter — a quote is a new post carrying its own text, which is precisely why quote-react is the reach engine.

CONFIRMED
Out-of-Network Discount

Posts from accounts a viewer does not follow are multiplied by a factor below 1. Reaching new people starts from a penalty — which is why the quality bar for a stranger-facing post is higher than for one your followers see.

CONFIRMED
Negative signals carry real negative weight

not_interested, mute_author, block_author, report — and not_dwelled. Scrolling past without stopping is itself a cost. Off-topic posts do not merely underperform; they actively subtract.

CONFIRMED
Two-tower retrieval on hashed author identity

Out-of-network candidates come from Phoenix embedding retrieval and SimClusters. Your author identity has to sit consistently inside one topic cluster to be retrievable by the right strangers. Spreading it across five — music, design-engineering, ai-tooling, web3 gaming, web3 design — blurs that embedding. That the mechanism exists is confirmed; that topical spread measurably dilutes it is this repo’s own topicConsistency hypothesis at medium confidence. The handful of political and crypto posts, at 5.3% of output, are not the cause.

HYPOTHESIS
Link suppression

This repo’s profile rates linkRisk at medium confidence and recommends putting links in the first reply. The published code does not name a link demotion. Your own data agrees with the hypothesis regardless — posts with an inline link averaged 20.5 likes against 36.5 without.

HYPOTHESIS
Early reply window

replyEarlyWindow, medium confidence: replying to a young, accelerating post rides its rising distribution, with velocity mattering more than total engagement. Already implemented in this repo’s reply-targets recipe at a 6-hour window.

NOT SUPPORTED
Blue checkmark as a ranking boost

Phoenix’s published feature list contains no verification or subscriber signal. The only subscription component in the pipeline is IneligibleSubscriptionFilter, which gates access to subscriber-only content and does nothing to ranking. Verification is not a demonstrated distribution lever. It buys longer posts, editing, and analytics — useful, but it will not fix reach, and nothing in your numbers should be blamed on its absence. Buy it as a tool, not as a remedy.
