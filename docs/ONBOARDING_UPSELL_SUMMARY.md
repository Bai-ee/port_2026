# Onboarding Upsell Summary

Date: 2026-06-19

## Purpose

Use this summary after a client completes onboarding or after you generate their first creative deliverables.

It is designed to do four things:

1. show that you organized their inputs
2. prove immediate value with visible assets
3. explain what the current package includes
4. open the door to higher-value managed initiatives

## What we can confidently position today

The strongest onboarding deliverables in the current product are:

- multi-device homepage captures
- device mockups
- social preview review
- motion / video mockup
- AI summary of what the site is starting from

These map cleanly to the current onboarding brief and verified Studio flow.

## Short client-facing version

Here is a polished summary you can use as-is:

> We’ve taken the information from your onboarding and organized it into a first-pass creative and presentation package. That includes a cross-device view of your site, a polished device mockup, a social share preview, a motion/video mockup, and a concise AI summary of what your current site is communicating today.  
>
> The value of this first deliverable is not just the visuals. It gives us a working baseline for how your brand appears across screens, how it shares socially, how it presents in motion, and where the current experience is strong or unclear. From there, we can turn the same inputs into higher-value next steps like a refined landing page, a stronger brand system, better launch assets, managed content, or a more complete growth strategy.

## Stronger upsell version

Use this when you want the handoff to feel more premium and managed:

> Your onboarding has now been turned into a managed creative baseline. We’ve taken your site, business context, goals, and positioning inputs and converted them into a visual review package: desktop/tablet/mobile presentation, a branded device mockup, social share presentation, and a motion-ready video mockup. We also generate a concise AI summary so the creative direction is grounded in what the site is actually saying right now.  
>
> This gives us more than a snapshot. It gives us a usable source of truth for what to improve next. Once we can see how the brand looks, shares, and reads across touchpoints, we can move into higher-value initiatives with much more clarity: brand identity refinement, landing page optimization, launch content, social rollout, conversion improvements, or an ongoing managed creative/growth retainer.

## “What we organized” section

Use this as a bullet block under the summary:

- Your current website and visible messaging
- Your onboarding answers: goals, blockers, priorities, and expectations
- Presentation across desktop, tablet, and mobile
- How your site appears when shared socially
- A motion/video mockup to show how the experience can be presented more dynamically
- A starting summary of what the brand is signaling today

## “What you received” section

This keeps the value concrete:

- Cross-device screenshots for layout review
- A polished device mockup for presentations and sales materials
- A social share preview so title/image/metadata presentation can be reviewed
- A motion mockup video for pitching, promotion, or launch content
- A summarized creative readout of the current experience

## “Why this matters” section

Use this immediately after the deliverables list:

> Most clients do not need more random ideas. They need their current presence organized into something they can review, react to, and build from. This onboarding package gives us that baseline. It shows what the market will actually see first, where the message is clear, where presentation breaks down, and where better creative or strategy work will have the most leverage.

## Best upsell paths

These are the most believable follow-on initiatives from the onboarding package.

### 1. Landing page or website refinement

Best when:

- the mockups look promising but the live site is weak
- the message is unclear
- mobile presentation is inconsistent

Position it like this:

> Now that we have the baseline across devices and in motion, the next step is tightening the real site so it matches the quality of the presentation assets.

### 2. Brand identity / visual system

Best when:

- the presentation feels inconsistent
- the site works but the brand is weak
- the client needs a more premium look across assets

Position it like this:

> The onboarding package shows us how the brand is currently appearing in the wild. A brand system initiative turns that from “good enough” into a repeatable visual language across the site, decks, social, and future campaigns.

### 3. Social launch kit

Best when:

- they want attention quickly
- the social preview and motion mockup create obvious momentum
- they need launch content but not a full rebuild yet

Position it like this:

> Since we already have the share preview and motion mockup, we can extend this into a launch kit: social posts, short-form promo assets, announcement creative, and reusable campaign visuals.

### 4. Managed content / growth support

Best when:

- they want ongoing help, not one-off assets
- they need direction each week
- they do not have an internal team to execute

Position it like this:

> The onboarding assets give us the baseline. A managed engagement turns that into a steady operating rhythm: updated creative, message refinement, launch support, social content, and ongoing recommendations based on what the business is trying to achieve.

### 5. Full strategy / knowledge capture

Best when:

- the client has lots of scattered information
- multiple offers, products, or stakeholders need alignment
- future work will depend on having cleaner source material

Position it like this:

> If the business has a lot of information spread across docs, calls, decks, and site pages, the next upgrade is turning that into a structured source of truth so every future brief, page, campaign, and asset starts from better inputs.

## Recommended value ladder

This is the cleanest upsell structure from the current onboarding package:

1. **Baseline Review**  
   Cross-device captures, device mockup, social preview, video mockup, AI summary

2. **Creative Upgrade**  
   Landing page refinement, better messaging, improved visual direction, launch assets

3. **Brand System**  
   Identity, visual consistency, reusable design rules, asset polish

4. **Launch / Content Package**  
   Social rollout, promo content, motion assets, campaign-ready creative

5. **Managed Growth Partner**  
   Ongoing strategy, creative support, site improvements, campaign iteration

## Best way to talk about “managed info”

Avoid vague language like “we manage your info.”

Use this instead:

> We turn your onboarding answers, site signals, visuals, and brand context into a usable working baseline. That gives us one place to manage what the business is saying, how it looks, and what should happen next.

Or:

> We organize your current site, business context, and presentation assets into a clear starting point so future design, content, and strategy work can build on something consistent.

## Suggested CTA lines

Pick one depending on how direct you want to be.

### Soft

> If you want, we can use this baseline to recommend the next highest-leverage initiative.

### Medium

> The next step is choosing whether we apply this baseline to the site itself, the brand system, or the launch content around it.

### Direct

> This onboarding package is the starting point. The real upside comes from turning it into a stronger site, a sharper brand system, and a managed rollout plan.

If you want one default version for email, PDF, or dashboard handoff, use this:

> We’ve turned your onboarding into a first-pass creative baseline built around what people actually see first. That includes your site across devices, a polished device mockup, a social share preview, a motion/video mockup, and a concise AI summary of how your current presence reads today.  
>
> This gives us a practical starting point for improvement. Instead of guessing, we can now see how the brand presents across screens, how it shares, and where the message or visuals need work. From here, the highest-value next steps are usually a stronger landing page, a clearer brand system, a social/content launch package, or a more fully managed creative and growth engagement.
