## Market Intelligence

**The market.** AI-era creative services for established small / local / creative businesses that have a real brand but no consistent content engine. The macro shift: AI has collapsed the price of commodity creative work (AI-first tools at $59–$109/mo do what a $1,500–$3,000/mo freelancer did; a 10-video campaign that cost $100k+ is now under $100). **Implication — compete on judgment, stewardship, and outcomes, never on per-asset price.**

**Category trends Bryan rides:**
- Rise of the **fractional creative director** (typ. $99k–$250k annualized; 2–4 clients; 6–12 month engagements; ~30% of time is brand stewardship) — the named role that matches his pitch.
- **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO/AEO)** demand surging — 31.3% of the US population using generative AI search in 2026; retainers $2k–$8k/mo, priced 20–40% above SEO; LLM-cited visitors close 5.7× faster.
- **Local/GBP** importance rising — 70%+ of local searches hit a Google Business Profile; review responses drive 68% more clicks; AI Overviews now name well-managed local businesses.
- Short-form **AI video** demand exploding (SMBs = 46% of AI-video signups) but commoditizing fast.

### Audience

- **Primary:** established small / local / creative businesses — credible brand, real website, visibly under-producing on social/video/promos, time-poor. Archetype: It's Raw Poke Shop (strong brand, no content engine).
- **Secondary:** other creatives, founders, and small studios who want a content system *stewarded*, not sold to them.
- **Motivations:** want consistency without doing it themselves; want to trust one accountable person; want to show up in AI/local search.
- **Objections:** "Can't I just buy the tool?" (reframe to curation + accountability); "Is this just AI slop?" (answer: human-owned, brand-stewarded); price vs cheap automation (answer: outcomes, not assets).
- **Platforms:** Instagram, X/Twitter, YouTube/TikTok (short-form), Google (local + AI search), LinkedIn (B2B/founder reach).

**Disqualifiers (wrong customers):** DIY tool-seekers and price-shoppers. Chasing them dilutes the position.

## Content Intelligence

### Voice

- **Tone summary:** Short, direct, tactical, systems-first. Low fluff, no generic agency language, no hype. Calm authority. Founder/operator-facing.
- **Writing rules:** lead with the outcome and the mechanism, not adjectives; one idea per line; let line breaks carry rhythm; concrete over clever; show the human and the accountability.
- **Preferred words:** system, steward, curate, durable, translation, accountable, consistent, baseline, judgment, ship.
- **Banned words:** transform, revolutionary, game-changing, synergy, unlock (as hype), "AI-powered" as a value claim, "10x", "guru".
- **Avoid patterns:** hype stacking, agency buzzwords, hands-off-automation-as-the-value, vague transformation promises, over-claiming reach/authority.

**Voice pillars (do / don't):**
- *Durability over hype* — Do: argue for year-three thinking. Don't: chase launch-day spectacle.
- *Systems over assets* — Do: frame the engine and the judgment. Don't: sell a single asset as the product.
- *Human accountability* — Do: say "I own the outcome." Don't: hide behind "the AI did it."
- *Translation, not redesign* — Do: frame legacy work as translation across surfaces. Don't: imply teardown.

### Content pillars

- Productized creative infrastructure — agency judgment encoded as a stewarded system.
- Human-in-the-loop automation — AI generates options; systems and the operator decide what survives.
- Durability over hype — building for year three, not launch day.
- Speed to proof — website in, deliverables out; before/after as the unit of trust.
- Systems-first operating discipline — single source of truth, repeatable wiring.

### Post examples (in-voice)

- *Manifesto:* "Most brands optimize for launch day. Few optimize for year three. Durability > hype."
- *Mechanism:* "AI generates options. Systems decide what survives."
- *Reframe:* "Modernizing legacy isn't redesign. It's translation."
- *Offer:* "Your website in. A full creative brief and a promo video out. In minutes — then I steward it from there."

### Platform styles

- **Twitter/X:** terse, line-broken aphorisms; build-in-public progress; mechanism > motivation.
- **LinkedIn:** the fractional-CD case — stewardship, accountability, outcomes for time-poor owners.
- **Instagram:** visual proof — before/after, brand systems in use, short promo clips.
- **Email digest voice:** calm operator briefing — what changed, what it means, what to do next.

**Recurring series (candidates):** "What the system caught this week" (stewardship in action); "Translation, not redesign" (legacy brand teardowns→translations); build-in-public log of letting his own social run on the curated system.
